        Before you go

          A personal walk guide, not an official park publication. Track lines are indicative — drawn by hand or simplified from a GPS trace — and will not keep you on the path in cloud. Carry a proper topographic map, and follow the signposts at every junction.

          Distances to stops are straight-line, so the walking distance is always longer. Hut and lift opening times change through the season and without notice.
